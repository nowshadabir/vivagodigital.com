The Challenge

Dewan Mohammod Salahuddin Babu is a figure with a vast and varied legacy. As a physician, a two-time Member of Parliament (BNP), an author of over 15 books, and a District Governor of Lions Clubs International, his life is a tapestry of service.

The challenge was to create a digital home that didn't just list these roles but wove them into a cohesive narrative, connecting him with constituents, readers, and patients alike.

The Solution

Vivago Digital engineered a multi-dimensional portfolio. We created distinct yet integrated sections to showcase his literary contributions, political journey, and social impact, all powered by a robust CMS for real-time updates.

Technical Excellence

Overcoming massive technical challenges to deliver a high-performance digital gallery

1.5TB Image Gallery

Managing Salahuddin's extensive photographic archive required a robust solution. We developed a custom admin panel that allows easy upload and organization of thousands of high-resolution images.

  • Custom CMS for bulk image uploads
  • Intelligent categorization & tagging system
  • Secure cloud storage with CDN integration

Custom Compression Engine

To maintain image quality while optimizing load times, we engineered a smart compression system that automatically processes images during upload.

  • Automatic WebP & AVIF conversion
  • Multi-resolution thumbnail generation
  • Reduced file sizes by up to 70%

Performance Optimization Challenge

With thousands of images, initial load times were unacceptable. Our team implemented a comprehensive performance strategy that transformed the user experience.
