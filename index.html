Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline depends on complexity. A standard corporate website usually takes 2-4 weeks, while custom SaaS applications can take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during our discovery phase.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Absolutely. We offer various retainer packages for maintenance, security updates, and feature additions to ensure your digital product evolves with your business.

What tech stack do you use?

We specialize in modern stacks: React, Next.js, and Node.js for web apps; Flutter for mobile; and robust cloud infrastructure via AWS and DigitalOcean.
